B.2
UP-IND-CCA Security.
Using the key insulation technique repeatedly, we prove the following result for
UP-IND-CCA security.
Theorem 7. Let UE be an updatable encryption scheme with deterministic re-
encryption. Suppose that UE has randomness-preserving re-encryption and simu-
latable token generation and the underlying encryption scheme SKE is tidy. Then
UE is UP-IND-CCA-secure if SKE is S-IND-CCA-secure.
