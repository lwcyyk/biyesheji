    Authors: Feiteng Nie, Yuan Sun, Shengkun Zhu, Sheng Wang, Zhiyong Peng

    Abstract: k-means provides a simple and efficient foundation for text clustering, but embedding alone can miss task-specific semantic boundaries.… ▽ More k-means provides a simple and efficient foundation for text clustering, but embedding alone can miss task-specific semantic boundaries. Must-link (ML) and cannot-link (CL) constraints can correct such errors, yet conventional constrained clustering assumes that these relations are already supplied. LLMs can generate them without task-specific labels, but exhaustive querying is costly and their judgments can be incorrect. We present GPKmeans, an efficient Group-Penalty k-means method for text clustering with noisy LLM-generated constraints. GPKmeans uses LLM as an offline local pairwise annotator, querying selected anchor-candidate pairs for sparse ML links and constructing set-valued CL evidence without all-pairs queries. Across nine datasets, GPKmeans uses fewer LLM calls and yields more accurate constraints. It achieves the best average ACC, NMI, and ARI while running approximately 30 times faster than state-of-the-art constrained clustering methods. △ Less

How energetic constraints shape synaptic precision and its regulation during plasticity remains unclear. Here we develop an energy--constrained framework in which synapses minimise postsynaptic response variance subject to a fixed mean and an effective energy budget. Combinations of candidate physiological costs are used to estimate an energy cost for synaptic transmission; this cost is then inferred from quantal statistics. Incorporating background knowledge into clustering to enhance solution quality and interpretability has attracted growing interest in mathematical optimization and machine learning research. Extending this paradigm to biclustering enables prior information to guide the joint grouping of rows and columns.
