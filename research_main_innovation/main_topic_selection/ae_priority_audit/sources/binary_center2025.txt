9   Conclusion

Wehaveinvestigatedthealgorithmiccomplexityof k-Center with Missing Entriesinthesetting
wherethemissingentriesaresparseandexhibitcertaingraph-theoreticstructure.Wehaveshownthat
theproblemisFPTwhenparameterizedby vc+k, fr+kand tw+k+d,where vcisthevertexcover
