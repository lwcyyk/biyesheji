In contrast, this paper formalises Decentralised Preference Discovery (DPD): the reliable identification of a social choice parameter (e.g. the canonical outcome of an aggregation rule applied to the global preference profile) under conditions of partial information, asynchronous interaction, censorship resistance, and no central coordinator. To address DPD, we propose Snowveil, a gossip-based framework where agents repeatedly sample random peer rankings and update local beliefs to converge on the canonical outcome.
