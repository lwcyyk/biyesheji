Managing invoices for your enterprise
You can view, pay, or download a current invoice for your enterprise, and you can view your payment history.
Copy markdown
Who can use this feature?
Enterprise owners and billing managers of invoiced customers of GitHub Enterprise.
Customers who pay directly via credit card, PayPal, or Azure subscription, should instead see Managing your payment and billing information
GitHub Enterprise Server customers with no enterprise account on GitHub.com should go to the GitHub Enterprise website
In this article
Viewing your current invoice
Paying your current invoice
Viewing your payment history and downloading any invoice
GitHub bills invoiced GitHub Enterprise customers through an enterprise account on GitHub.com. Each invoice includes a single bill charge for all of your paid GitHub.com services and any GitHub Enterprise Server instances. For more information, see Billing for GitHub Enterprise.
Viewing your current invoice
Navigate to GitHub Enterprise Cloud.
In the top-right corner of GitHub, click your profile picture.
Depending on your environment, click Enterprise, or click Enterprises then click the enterprise you want to view.
At the top of the page, click Billing and licensing.
On the "Overview" page, in the "Latest invoice" section, click View invoice to open a PDF of your latest invoice.
Tip
If all your invoices are paid, the "Latest invoice" section is labeled "Estimated next payment". To view paid invoices, in the sidebar, click Past invoices.
To understand the components of your invoice, see Viewing usage for your GitHub Enterprise plan and Viewing your usage of metered products and licenses.
Paying your current invoice
On the "Overview" page, in the "Latest invoice" section, click Pay now to display a secure payment dialog.
Type your credit card information in the secure form, then click Pay invoice to pay the amount due.
Viewing your payment history and downloading any invoice
In the sidebar of the Billing and licensing tab, click Past invoices to show details of your past invoices.
In the "REFERENCE" column, click the reference number of an invoice that you want to download.
