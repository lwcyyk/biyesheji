Viewing and managing your sessions
You can view and revoke your active sessions in your settings.
Copy markdown
You can view a list of devices that have logged into your account, and revoke any sessions that you don't recognize.
In the upper-right corner of any page on GitHub, click your profile picture, then click Settings.
In the "Access" section of the sidebar, click Sessions.
Under "Web sessions", you can see your active web sessions.
Under "GitHub Mobile sessions", you can see a list of devices that have logged into your account via the GitHub Mobile app.
To see the web session details, click See more.
To revoke a web session, click Revoke session.
Optionally, to revoke a GitHub Mobile session, go back to the Sessions overview page and click Revoke next to the device you want to revoke.
Note
Revoking a mobile session signs you out of the GitHub Mobile application on that device and removes it as a second-factor option.
