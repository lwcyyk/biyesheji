Vita

Born in 1963 in (Eastern) Germany, married, two children.
Studied mathematics in Halle and Jena, 1991 Dr. rer. nat.
1991-2000 assistant at FernUniversität Hagen.
1994 Habilitation in computer science.
Since 2000 here at Chalmers.

Interests: classical music, cooking, art, philosophy, literature, satire, history, and ... :
