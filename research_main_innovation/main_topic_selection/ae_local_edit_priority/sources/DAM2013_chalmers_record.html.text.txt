Cluster editing with locally bounded modifications revisited

Paper i proceeding, 2013



For Cluster Editing where both the number of clusters and the edit degree are bounded, we speed up the kernelization by almost a factor n compared to Komusiewicz and Uhlmann (2012), at cost of a marginally worse kernel size bound. We also give sufficient conditions for a subset of vertices to be a 
cluster in some optimal clustering.
