Our approach constructs reusable, schema-based page representations and identifies coordination actions through correspondences across schemas. We instantiate this approach in Cling, which surfaces coordination opportunities in situ for users to select, configure, and execute incrementally while directing the broader task. A technical evaluation demonstrates accurate identification of useful opportunities across diverse domains. A 12-participant study found that Cling reduced task durations by more than half compared with both manual interaction and an end-to-end AI agent, while requiring 61% fewer clicks and 80% fewer tab switches than manual interaction. Its small-step automation supported control and verification as actions unfolded, while accommodating intent as it emerged and evolved through information encountered on the page. △ Less
