Intuition              of               Our               Security              Notion.         The         notion         is         an         extension         of         prior-work
notions          [11,  12]          which          we          augment          with          the          introduction          of          expiry          epochs.          (We
later          show         that          our         notion          implies          such         prior-work          notions.)          The         distinguishing
feature           of           our           model           is           that           we           allow           the           adversary           to           query           the           update           token
in        an        epoch        which        corresponds        to        the        challenge-ciphertext        expiry        epoch        eexp         and
allow        that        the        key        in        eexp     +1        can        be        retrieved.        In        models        without        expiry        epochs,
leaking           the           epoch-e           challenge           ciphertext           and           an           epoch-e           token           together           with           a
key           in           ep o ch         e     +1         would          yield          a         trivial          win          (per         deﬁnition          of         correctness).
